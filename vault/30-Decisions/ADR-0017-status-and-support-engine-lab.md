# ADR-0017 Status and support engine lab

- **Status:** accepted for comparative prototypes, not converged balance.
- **Reopens/amends:** B6 and B8's deferred elemental combinations. The user
  explicitly requests support-tower engineering, rare stacking effects and
  repeated independent driver/judge review. Art and meta progression stay deferred.
- **Decision:** keep three data-selected experiments in one deterministic
  client: Raid/Heat, compounding investment, and low-core conditional pacts.
  Add poison, global slow and enemy confusion to the rare-drop pool alongside
  death-chain explosions. Add water delivery for lightning/slow combinations
  and consumable flammable oil for thermal burst/burn combinations.
- **Stacking:** preserve large multiplicative build payoffs, but apply the
  small extra duplicate kicker only to additive status/explosion effects.
  Poison derives from real hits, stacks to a data-defined dose ceiling, and
  cannot gain a minimum damage on every tick. Confusion has a bounded cadence,
  requires another living enemy, and cannot recursively trigger on-hit effects.
  Slow has a floor; death cleanup pays each bounty exactly once.
- **Choice costs:** rerolls spend real gold, escalate within an offer and reset
  only at the next offer. Seeded future waves/drops remain independent. A support
  purchase must compete with another damage tower or an upgrade at a real
  checkpoint; no free diagnostic resources in ordinary-run witnesses.
- **Driver discipline:** use public previews and legal commands. Exact next-wave
  forecasts are explicitly oracle-assisted, never human intelligence. Preserve
  existing kill-zone coverage when mazing. Frozen-defense and adaptive choice
  forks must distinguish bad decisions from bad loot or insufficient power.
- **Alternatives:** more generic damage cards (weak identities); uncapped enemy-
  HP poison (base DPS becomes irrelevant); free rerolls (no opportunity cost);
  unconditional universal slow multiplication (unreadable permanent stalls).
- **Consequences:** optional rules/state preserve old embedded replay behavior.
  Each candidate has frozen configs, replay witnesses and a diary verdict.
  The stopping standard is evidence-backed readiness for a human fun trial,
  not a claim that simulations experience enjoyment or certify human fun.
- **Rewind:** select retained prior embedded rules or remove optional mechanics
  from new-run data. Never overwrite old witnesses to match a newer driver.
